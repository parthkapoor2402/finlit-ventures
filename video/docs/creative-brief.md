# Creative brief — Before You Decide

## Direction

A quiet evening, then the moment before a SIP change is confirmed. Skip Check is a separate contribution comparison. The relief is understanding, and the decision stays with the person.

Working title: Before You Decide.
End line: See the difference. Make your decision.
Brand line: Skip Check by FinLit Ventures.

## What the film may say

- A person may consider a change because of market worry, a cash need, or a goal that has moved.
- Their own investing app is where the change is finished.
- Skip Check shows `(current − proposed) × months` for one SIP.
- The worked reduction is ₹12,000 to ₹6,000 over 6 months, which is ₹36,000.
- A pause of ₹12,000 for those 6 months is ₹72,000. Both figures are labeled.
- Existing units stay invested. Future units are not estimated. The tool does not change or restart the SIP.
- Keep, Reduce, and Pause are equal. Pause is shown as an accepted choice, not the right choice.
- FinLit states it is not registered with SEBI or AMFI. This is decision support, not investment advice.

## What the film does not say

No named broker integration, no claim that another app is broken, no return forecast, no “you should keep investing,” and no AI claim. The opening photograph is an original still of a person at home. The phone screens are drawn for this film.

## Scene order

1. Evening still and the line that plans change.
2. Market worry beside a household bill.
3. The gap before confirmation: make the change, or understand the difference.
4. Skip Check entry, first door selected.
5. Reasons, with cash need shown before market worry is selected.
6. The ₹36,000 reduction.
7. What that number does not mean.
8. Equal choices, then a pause, then the comprehension lines.
9. Recap, then back to the evening. The person stays in control.
10. End card.

Timing follows the voiceover. The cut is about 123 seconds.
