# Tata Neu — weekly student job

No concept can credibly create two or three meaningful Tata Neu opens in an ordinary college week. This is a non-build. The full evidence note is `tata-neu-weekly-job-strategy.md` in the same folder.

## 1. Why NeuRoom failed

A held product is an event. In a week where nobody is choosing a phone, a laptop, a ticket, or a wardrobe item, the room has nothing true to show. A habit product that is dark in the weeks the business calls dormant does not fix dormancy. A price tick, a coin, or a streak would only hide that hole.

## 2. Why NeuSlip is still insufficient

Open 1 is one intentional visit. Open 2 waits on another person. Open 3 is not promised, and a price change is now an illegal reason for it. Neu does not publicly know who shares a room, and the coordination job already lives in WhatsApp. It stays rejected. It is not renamed.

## 3. True recurring student jobs

Seven jobs, each with an owner:

| Job | What recurs | Who already finishes it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Get to the teaching day | National Statistics Office travel survey: students 15–29 have the highest regular education-trip rate. IIT Indore and IIT Bombay teach Monday–Saturday. | Feet, bus, college timetable |
| Eat | Vaidya et al. (n=943): students living alone order about 4× more often than those with parents. Dhanith et al., Pune (n=200): a few times a week 38%, daily 30.5%. | Mess, Swiggy (46.5%), Zomato (43%) |
| Pay a small campus thing | Tauro & Rose, Mangaluru (n=100 existing UPI users): daily 48%, several times a week 43%; food/canteen/college 84%. | Google Pay 78%, PhonePe 15% |

| Do the academic work | Time Use Survey: learning participation 29.5% on the reference day. Exam weeks are spikes, not the ordinary week. | College tools, YouTube |

## 4. Student weekly journey

India does not publish a Monday–Sunday college diary. On the two cited IIT calendars, Monday–Saturday is a teaching day: a short commute, class, and food from the mess, the canteen, or a delivery app. Sunday drops the academic object. Food, chat, and media do not drop. An exam fortnight is a different calendar. The week is not three Tata consideration events. Bain’s Gen Z shopping findings and the BCG/Snap research-intensity finding describe episodic purchase journeys.

## 5. Competitive whitespace

The jobs that recur are occupied. A Neu feature on any of them is a nearby-store notification, a food marketplace, a second UPI app, a chat, a content layer, or an extra hop in front of a brand app. The empty space is Tata’s ignorance of college, hostel, and roommates. That is not a missing student feature.

## 6. Tata ecosystem opportunity

Croma, Westside, BigBasket/bbnow, 1mg, Qmin, and Neu UPI are real. Zudio is not on Neu. Air India NeuCoin redemption is excluded in HDFC Tata Neu card terms. 1 NeuCoin equals ₹1 only where the brand allows it. None of these is a non-purchase object the student needs three times in an ordinary week. FY26 points Neu at financial services and loyalty attached to spend. Tata Digital’s FY26 loss was ₹4,974 crore on ₹46,515 crore GMV.

## 7–8. Five concepts, and why each dies

| Concept | The three moments | Why it fails |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hunger Door | Hunger, then the next hunger, then a further hunger | The third meal is real for students living away from home. It is Swiggy or Zomato. It is also a purchase. |
| Visible Rupee | Check the week’s money, then check it after a spend, then check it again | The payments that change the number sit in Google Pay. Neu does not see them. |
| Third Class | A class the student already planned, then the next, then a third | Three paid sessions a week are unmeasured. For everyone else the third open is a reminder. |
| Broken Kit | Something broke, then a second look in that same episode | Strongest Tata story in the set, and still an event. A quiet week has no open. |
| Parent Ping | Money arrives | Open 2 waits on a parent. This is NeuSlip with a different cast. |

No hybrid repairs this. The jobs that could move a weekly count do not open Neu. The jobs that can open Neu do not happen weekly.

## 10–16. Winner, opens, habit

There is no product. There is no first, second, or third open to design.

The habit loops that exist are already closed. Hunger returns and Swiggy, Zomato, or the mess closes it. A small payment returns and Google Pay closes it in the only student sample cited. The circle returns and WhatsApp closes it. Tata’s public loop is spend, then a NeuCoin where the brand allows it, then silence until the next spend.

## 17–19. Moat, data, business

The moat is a rupee-denominated loyalty unit on eligible brands, a co-branded card, a Croma store network, a grocery business, and a pharmacy. It appears at checkout. A checkout moat cannot be the reason the app was opened on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday of a week with no checkout.

Neu can see Tata transactions it processed. It does not see Google Pay, PhonePe, Swiggy, WhatsApp, or the mess. The interesting student week is the week those other systems see.

No new revenue model. Empty opens do not pay down that loss. Coins for opening are out.

## 20–25. Metrics, experiment, kill, 30 days

**L0** is youth WAU versus control, bar +15%. Current youth WAU is `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA`.

**L1** is meaningful opens per youth user per ordinary week: a session the user can explain, which still exists if the push, the coin, the price tick, the other person, and the streak are removed.

**L2** is open-to-shop on those sessions. It is the bonus. It is not a substitute for L0.

Do not A/B a feature. For 30 days, pull eight ordinary weeks of youth sessions, including the zeros, and run 40 fourteen-day diaries (20 living with parents, 20 living away), recruited outside Neu, with no prototype and no “you could use Neu for that.”

Kill the program if the mass of sessions sits at zero or one, if the diaries show class, food, UPI, and chat in other apps, if Tata appears only as a purchase, or if the only way to force a third session is a push, a coin, a price change, another person, or a streak. A completed diary with zero Tata moments is a successful validation of the non-build.

## 26–27. Panel attack and recommendation

A panel that still wants a feature has to name an ordinary-week object the student needs on three separate occasions, show the third occasion in a diary, name the app that already finishes it, and explain a week in which the student is fed, paid, in class, and nothing is broken. If the honest answer is that Neu stays closed, the product does not meet the brief.

Do not build. Do not fund Hunger Door because 50.8 beat 36.4. Spend the next 30 days on the log pull and the 40 diaries. If they reproduce this note, close the brief. If they surface one job that passes the kill rules, write one narrow test for that job and nothing else.
