# Tata Neu — weekly student job

No concept in this note can credibly create two or three meaningful Tata Neu opens in an ordinary college week. The highest-scoring idea, Hunger Door, scores 50.8 out of 100 and still fails the gate. The recurring jobs in a student week already have an owner. Tata Neu’s public assets show up when someone is buying a Tata product, which is an occasional event. This is an explicit non-build. The work that follows is a research program, not a feature, and it is not a renamed slip.

This note does not design, prototype, or code anything. It does not defend NeuRoom or NeuSlip. Frequencies, weekly-active-user rates, conversion, price-change rates, APIs, inventory, a student graph, and Google Pay transaction visibility are omitted wherever they were not published. Where a number is missing, the line says exactly `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH` or `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA`.

Tags: **[EVIDENCE]** is a source. **[INFERENCE]** is a conclusion from those sources. **[HYPOTHESIS]** is untested. **[ASSUMPTION]** is something a build would need and does not have. **[RECOMMENDATION]** is a decision. Small samples are not national rates. Scores in section 9 are hostile judgements. Only the arithmetic is calculated.

## 1. Why NeuRoom failed

[EVIDENCE] The case states that youth use of Tata Neu is event-driven: a student opens for a considered purchase and then the app goes quiet. [INFERENCE] NeuRoom took that event and tried to stretch it across a week by holding a product in view until the price, the stock, or the group moved.

[INFERENCE] A product consideration is not a weekly object. In a week where nobody is choosing a phone, a laptop, a ticket, or a wardrobe item, the room has nothing true to show. The prior rule that quiet weeks stay quiet was an honest description of that emptiness. It is also the reason the concept cannot produce two or three opens in an ordinary week. [RECOMMENDATION] That conflict is fatal. A habit product that is dark in the weeks the business calls dormant does not fix dormancy.

[HYPOTHESIS] Nobody has published how often a held student SKU actually changes price or stock inside a seven-day window. That rate is `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH` on the student side and `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA` on the catalogue side. Using an unmeasured price change as the reason to come back is the failure mode this brief now forbids. A notification, a coin, or a streak would only hide the same hole.

[INFERENCE] NeuRoom also had no right to the student’s week. Neu does not publicly know the college, the hostel, or the roommates. The circle that researches a purchase, which BCG and Snap described on 16 October 2024 as Gen Z researching about 1.5 times harder and deciding with a small circle, is a purchase journey. It is not a Monday-to-Sunday calendar. [RECOMMENDATION] Kill NeuRoom as a weekly-open bet. Keep the underlying fact it stumbled on: when a Tata product is already being decided, a true price and a true redemption rule matter. That fact is a resolver during a purchase. It is not a reason to open on a quiet Wednesday.

## 2. Why NeuSlip is still insufficient

[RECOMMENDATION] NeuSlip stays rejected. It is not renamed here, and “unresolved weekly slip” is not the winner.

[INFERENCE] Open 1 was the only plausible session. A student can start a list of what is still undecided, or can reopen something left from last week. That is one intentional visit. It does not require a coin.

[INFERENCE] Open 2 depended on another person. Someone else had to add a line, vote, or change a cost. A second session that exists because another person happened to act is the dependency this brief rejects. If the roommate does nothing, the week has one open.

[INFERENCE] Open 3 was not promised. It appeared only if a further state change existed, such as a price, a stock line, or a weekend close that still carried new information. A price change is now an illegal reason for the third session. A weekend close that adds no new information is a ritual. [HYPOTHESIS] The chance that a real circle produces three state changes in an ordinary week, with no push and no coin, is `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`. The prior concept did not claim that chance was high.

[INFERENCE] NeuSlip also inherited NeuRoom’s data hole. Neu does not publicly know who shares a room. The slip asked students to move a coordination job out of WhatsApp into an app they do not already use for coordination. [EVIDENCE] Economic Times, 21 April 2026, quotes Tata Digital CEO Sajith Sivanandan: Indians spend more than 70 percent of their time in their top five apps, and Neu should complement brand apps rather than compete for traffic. WhatsApp is already one of those apps in the youth media evidence below. [RECOMMENDATION] A shared slip cannot be the weekly product. Do not fund a variant that waits on a roommate, a parent, or a price tick.

## 3. True recurring student jobs

The unit is the college week, starting from the student’s day. Tata categories come afterwards, and only as a test of whether Tata can finish the job.

### Job 1 — Get to the teaching day

**JOB.** Leave where you sleep, reach the institution, and attend what the timetable requires.

**FREQUENCY.** [EVIDENCE] National Household Travel Survey, July 2025 to June 2026, National Statistics Office: a trip is travel of more than 1 km. Students aged 15–29 record the highest regular education-trip participation, 79.1 percent in rural India and 73.3 percent in urban India. Among students aged 6 and above, 65.6 percent rural and 68.1 percent urban make such a trip. This cut is “students,” which includes school. A college-only weekly count is `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`. [EVIDENCE] The 2026–27 academic calendars of IIT Indore and IIT Bombay schedule teaching across Monday to Saturday, use Saturday for exams or for classes missed on a holiday, and treat Sunday as outside the regular teaching grid. That is two institutes. The national college week is `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`.

**CURRENT APP.** The timetable and the gate. Maps or a transit app when the route is unfamiliar. WhatsApp when the room or the cancellation changes. [HYPOTHESIS] Which app owns “class cancelled” on a given campus is `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`.

**WORKAROUND.** Walk, bus, bicycle, auto, or two-wheeler. [EVIDENCE] In the same travel survey, student commutes nationwide are walking 37.0 percent, buses 29.5 percent, bicycles 15.8 percent, auto-rickshaws 10.5 percent (14.4 percent urban), and two-wheelers 6.7 percent (11.5 percent urban). Average travel time is 23 minutes. Urban students spend ₹617 a month on the education trip and rural students ₹361.

**FRICTION.** [EVIDENCE] An IIT Kanpur campus survey reported by Vox Populi, 457 respondents, found 260 attending all or most classes, 108 attending around quizzes and important topics, and 85 rarely attending. Early classes were named by 314 of 421 respondents on that item, and 56 percent said they attend to the percentage the grading rule requires. One campus. Do not generalise.

**WHY RETURN.** The timetable returns on the next teaching day without anyone sending a push.

**WHY TATA COULD.** Tata has no public student transit product. A Croma or a Westside on a route is a shop the student may pass. Passing a shop is not the job.

**WHY TATA SHOULD NOT.** Opening Tata Neu does not get the student into the room. A “you are near a store” prompt is a notification, which this brief rejects as a third session.

### Job 2 — Eat, including the meal the mess loses

**JOB.** Get the next meal or snack when hungry, from the mess, the canteen, a kitchen, or a delivery app.

**FREQUENCY.** [EVIDENCE] Vaidya, Patil, and Vaidya, IJIRT, February 2025: convenience and snowball sample of 943 college students aged 18–21 who already use food-delivery apps. Students living alone in a flat, PG, or hostel were about four times as likely to order multiple times a week or daily as students living with parents. Dinner was the usual slot. App users only. Not a national rate. [EVIDENCE] Dhanith and colleagues, IJFMR, Pune, convenience sample of 200, fielded February 2025: a few times a week 38 percent, daily 30.5 percent, multiple times a day 14.5 percent. Swiggy 46.5 percent, Zomato 43 percent. City-level, and only people already in this behaviour. [EVIDENCE] A Pune hostel study using the theory of planned behaviour (online questionnaire, August 2023, 321 complete responses from hostel students) finds that attitude and peer norms are associated with skipping mess meals. It does not publish skips per week. Skips per ordinary week are `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`.

**CURRENT APP.** Swiggy and Zomato in the Pune sample. The mess, where the hostel runs one. At Manipal Academy of Higher Education Bengaluru’s 2025–26 hostel rules, mess food is tied to a facial-recognition session and members can change mess service only in a short window each month. One institution’s rulebook, not a market.

**WORKAROUND.** Canteen, outside food, food from home, or cooking where a kitchen exists. Kitchen access by living arrangement is `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`.

**FRICTION.** Mess quality and peer norms in the Pune hostel study. Delivery price, stockouts, and late-night availability for students are described in a 2025 Jain University student quick-commerce paper, but that paper’s own tabulated order set is about 32 orders and its sampling frame is not stated. Its headline that “over 70 percent of non-local students order 3–5 times a week” is not used. Student delivery fees and willingness to pay are `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`.

**WHY RETURN.** Hunger returns on its own. The next meal does not need a notification, a coin, a price change, another person, or a streak.

**WHY TATA COULD.** [EVIDENCE] Tata Neu’s public site lists bbnow for grocery and Qmin for gourmet food. BigBasket is inside the Tata Digital portfolio. [HYPOTHESIS] Whether those surfaces stock the canteen-priced meal a hostel student actually wants, at the hour they want it, is `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA` and `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`.

**WHY TATA SHOULD NOT.** The evidenced meal apps are Swiggy and Zomato. [EVIDENCE] Sivanandan told Economic Times on 21 April 2026 that commerce-as-super-app probably did not work, and that Neu should complement BigBasket, 1mg, Croma, and the other brand surfaces rather than compete with them for traffic. A meal product inside Neu is a second window on a job a faster app already finishes. Building that window is a Swiggy clone, which this brief forbids.

### Job 3 — Pay for a small campus thing

**JOB.** Settle a canteen bill, a college fee, a recharge, or a small shared cost at the moment it is due.

**FREQUENCY.** [EVIDENCE] Tauro and Rose, IJIRT, Mangaluru: 100 students aged 18–23 who had already used UPI for six months. Daily 48 percent, several times a week 43 percent. Stated main purpose food, canteen, and college 84 percent. Google Pay 78 percent, PhonePe 15 percent. Tata Neu is not in that preference list. Not national. [EVIDENCE] NPCI does not publish youth transactions per user. A national youth UPI frequency is not available. Do not invent one. How often a college student pays specifically through Tata Neu is `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA`.

**CURRENT APP.** Google Pay, then PhonePe, in that Mangaluru sample of existing UPI users.

**WORKAROUND.** Cash. The cash share among college students is `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`.

**FRICTION.** [EVIDENCE] 2025–26 Reddit threads, mostly r/CreditCardsIndia and cardholders rather than students, describe Tata Neu as slow and Neu UPI as failing or timing out. Qualitative. A failure rate is `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA`.

**WHY RETURN.** The next canteen or college payment is its own event. In the Mangaluru sample those events bunch inside a week for people who already use UPI.

**WHY TATA COULD.** [EVIDENCE] Tata Neu offers UPI and bill payment. The Tata Sons FY26 annual report says Neu is refocused on financial services and loyalty and aims to increase payments monthly transacting users by 10 times. That is a company target, not a student behaviour.

**WHY TATA SHOULD NOT.** The students in the only cited UPI sample already complete this job in Google Pay. Asking them to open Neu in order to earn coins on a payment they can finish elsewhere makes the coin the reason for the session. Coins-for-opening, including coins-for-paying-in-our-rail-so-that-you-open, are out. Neu also cannot see the Google Pay transactions that make the week look busy. Visibility of those transactions is `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA` only in the sense that Tata must confirm the wall; publicly, another app’s UPI log is not Neu’s.

### Job 4 — Stay in the small circle

**JOB.** Know what the class, the flat, or the family decided: a room change, a note, a plan, a “come down.”

**FREQUENCY.** [EVIDENCE] Time Use Survey 2024, NSO, persons aged 15–29, all-India, one reference day: participation in socialising, communication, community participation, and religious practice is 90.6 percent. Average time per person in that band is 116 minutes. Participation in culture, leisure, mass media, and sports is 92.9 percent, 156 minutes per person. These bands mix conversation, religion, media, and sport. They are not WhatsApp opens, and the age band is not “college students.” College messaging frequency is `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`. [EVIDENCE] YouGov India Media Morph, April 2024, urban Profiles: social media is the top news source for Gen Z (24 percent), Instagram is the top social network they belong to, and 30 percent of Gen Z spend 11 or more hours a week on social media. Urban, not a college diary. [EVIDENCE] IJCRT survey of students at central universities in the North East, 570 responses: WhatsApp was the preferred platform for 45 percent, YouTube 24 percent, Instagram 23 percent; 37 percent reported 3–5 hours a day on social media. Regional, not national.

**CURRENT APP.** WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube.

**WORKAROUND.** Calling, or walking to the room. The split between chat and in-person coordination is `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`.

**FRICTION.** [EVIDENCE] A separate college study-habits paper (the extract reports social media as a study distraction for 68.9 percent) is consistent with attention sitting in social apps. Sample frame of that paper is not relied on for a national rate. Distraction is the competitor for any new Neu session.

**WHY RETURN.** The circle keeps talking. The next message is independent of Tata.

**WHY TATA COULD.** Tata has no student social graph. There is nothing public to attach a circle to.

**WHY TATA SHOULD NOT.** A chat, feed, or group board inside Neu is a WhatsApp or Instagram clone. Sivanandan’s point about the top five apps applies directly. Neu would be asking for time the student has already allocated.

### Job 5 — Do the academic work when it is actually due

**JOB.** Attend to classes, readings, assignments, labs, or an exam, on the days the course requires it.

**FREQUENCY.** [EVIDENCE] Time Use Survey 2024: among all persons aged 15–29, learning participation on the reference day is 29.5 percent, and average learning time per person is 124 minutes. Among those who did learn, time per participant is 422 minutes. This age band includes people who are not in college. Employment participation in the same band is 35.3 percent. [INFERENCE] A random 15–29-year-old is often not in a learning day. College assignment frequency, and how many deadlines fall in an ordinary week versus an exam fortnight, is `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`. [EVIDENCE] IIT Bombay’s 2026–27 calendar clusters mid-semester and end-semester exams, and includes Saturdays inside exam dates. Exam load is a spike on top of the week, not the week itself.

**CURRENT APP.** The college’s own classroom tool, plus WhatsApp groups and YouTube. Which official tool dominates is `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`.

**WORKAROUND.** Notes, library, all-night study before the exam. A medical-student time study and a small college time-management survey both show wide spreads in study hours. They are not used here as a weekly rate.

**FRICTION.** The work is lumpy. A quiet teaching week can contain classes and still contain no artefact a commerce app can help with.

**WHY RETURN.** The next class or the next deadline. Both are set by the institution.

**WHY TATA COULD.** Almost never, on public evidence. A charger, a laptop, or a medicine is a Broken Kit event (section 7). It is not the assignment.

**WHY TATA SHOULD NOT.** Tata has no academic object. Wrapping a deadline in a deal, a trivia question, or a “10 things you might like” tray manufactures a session the student did not need.

### Job 6 — Replenish the room, for students living away from home

**JOB.** Restock snacks, a drink, a personal-care item, or a grocery staple when the room runs out.

**FREQUENCY.** [EVIDENCE] IJSREM, March 2026, online questionnaire of 384 Gen Z respondents in Tier-1 cities, not a college frame and not a Tata Neu frame: quick-commerce use multiple times a week 31.0 percent, once a week 21.9 percent, one to three times a month 26.3 percent, less than once a month 16.1 percent, never 4.4 percent. [INFERENCE] Among this Tier-1 Gen Z sample, a large minority already has a multi-visit quick-commerce week. [HYPOTHESIS] The share of college students, by hostel versus parents, who do this on BigBasket or bbnow is `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`. Brand split inside that job is `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA` for Tata’s own logs and `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH` for Blinkit, Zepto, and Instamart.

**CURRENT APP.** Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, and BigBasket’s own quick-commerce surface. Student preference shares are `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`.

**WORKAROUND.** The campus store, a kirana, or a parcel from home. How often those substitute for an app is `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`.

**FRICTION.** Small baskets and delivery charges worry students in the Jain paper’s commentary. Amounts are not reused, because the underlying order table is too small to trust.

**WHY RETURN.** The item runs out. That can be a second or third occasion in a week for the heavy users in the Tier-1 Gen Z sample. It is a purchase occasion.

**WHY TATA COULD.** BigBasket is a Tata Digital business, and bbnow is named on tataneu.com. The CEO told Economic Times that BigBasket has been rebuilding quick commerce after losing ground to Blinkit, Zepto, and Instamart, and he cited “north of 800 stores and fulfilment centres” as management’s infrastructure claim. That is his account of the asset, not a student study.

**WHY TATA SHOULD NOT.** The replenishment habit, where it exists, lives in the quick-commerce app the student already trusts. Routing it through Neu adds a hop the CEO has said the company should stop asking for. The third session is another order. The case asked for meaningful opens without a purchase. An order is a purchase.

### Job 7 — Use the body the campus already provides

**JOB.** Walk, play, or train, because the day has a gap or because the student already trains.

**FREQUENCY.** A weekly count of college students who book three paid fitness sessions is `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`. Single-campus nutrition studies of hostel students mention games and exercise. They are not a national rate and they are not app opens. They are not used as a frequency.

**CURRENT APP.** Often no app. The college ground or the hostel gym. Cult.fit exists as a paid alternative; its college penetration and its price relative to pocket money are `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`.

**WORKAROUND.** The free facility on campus.

**FRICTION.** Money, and the fact that a paid class is a second system next to a free one.

**WHY RETURN.** The next session, if a real training plan exists. For a student with no plan, nothing returns.

**WHY TATA COULD.** [EVIDENCE] Cult appears on the HDFC Tata Neu partner-brand list. A deep link is technically imaginable. Whether Neu can book a Cult class without sending the student into the Cult app is `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA`.

**WHY TATA SHOULD NOT.** The class, when it is real, is the Cult product or the campus gym. Neu as a third app in front of Cult repeats the super-app hop Sivanandan rejected. A streak of opens around a class the student did not already want is forbidden.

## 4. Student weekly journey

[INFERENCE] India does not publish a Monday-to-Sunday diary of college students. The grid below uses the teaching calendars, the travel survey, and the food and payments samples. Anything placed on a named weekday beyond “teaching day versus Sunday” is marked as inference. Day-of-week peaks for food orders, UPI, and quick commerce are `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`.

**Monday to Saturday, teaching days at the two cited IITs.** [EVIDENCE] Those calendars run instruction on weekdays and Saturday. [INFERENCE] For a student on a similar grid, the day starts with a commute that the travel survey says is often under 30 minutes, then classes, with food somewhere in the mess, the canteen, or an app. [EVIDENCE] In the Mangaluru UPI sample, food, canteen, and college payments are the stated main use. [INFERENCE] Some of those payments sit on teaching days. Which ones, and whether they cluster at lunch, is unmeasured. [EVIDENCE] Time Use Survey minutes for ages 15–29 put self-care at 694 minutes per person on the reference day, before learning, paid work, or leisure. [INFERENCE] Most of a young person’s day is not a commerce session. Treating every gap as a Neu opportunity invents demand.

**A heavy food day, living away from parents.** [EVIDENCE] Vaidya and colleagues: living alone lines up with ordering multiple times a week or daily, among students who already use food apps. Dhanith and colleagues: in a Pune convenience sample, daily and several-times-a-week ordering are both common, on Swiggy and Zomato. [INFERENCE] On those days the second and third food moments are real for that subset. They are real inside someone else’s app. They are not evidence of a second and third Tata Neu open.

**Sunday, on the two IIT calendars.** [EVIDENCE] Regular teaching excludes Sunday. [INFERENCE] The academic object goes quiet. Food, chat, and media do not. What students buy on Sunday is `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`. A Sunday Neu “weekend edit” would be a deal tray.

**Exam fortnight.** [EVIDENCE] IIT Bombay dates mid-semester and end-semester exams as blocks, with Saturdays included. [INFERENCE] Those weeks are a different calendar. They can create a Broken Kit or a snack spike. They do not describe the ordinary week the case is trying to fill. Designing for exam week and calling it a weekly habit repeats NeuRoom: the product wakes for an event and sleeps afterwards.

**What the week is not.** [INFERENCE] It is not three Tata consideration events. Bain’s How India Shops Online 2025 finding that Gen Z is about 40 percent of e-retail shoppers, that about half use five or more platforms, that about 70 percent discover brands online, and that more than 90 percent of Gen Z digital payers prefer UPI, describes shopping and payment rails. It does not describe a weekly Tata Neu visit. BCG and Snap, 16 October 2024, describe a heavier research journey with a small circle. That journey is episodic.

## 5. Competitive whitespace

[INFERENCE] The jobs that actually recur are occupied.

| Job that recurs | Who finishes it today | What a Neu feature would be |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Teaching-day commute and attendance | The student’s feet, the bus, the college timetable | A notification that a store is nearby |
| The next meal | Mess, canteen, Swiggy, Zomato | A food marketplace |
| The small payment | Google Pay, PhonePe, in the only student UPI sample cited | A second UPI app, sweetened with coins |
| The circle | WhatsApp, Instagram | A chat or a feed |
| The assignment | The college and YouTube | A content or trivia layer |
| Room replenishment, where it is weekly | Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart, and BigBasket’s own app | A hop in front of a brand app the CEO said to leave alone |
| Paid fitness, for the unknown subset who buy it | Cult’s own app, or the free campus gym | A hop in front of Cult |

[INFERENCE] The whitespace that is actually empty is Tata’s ignorance, not a missing student feature. Neu does not publicly know college, hostel, or roommates. Nobody in the public sources has shown an ordinary college week in which a student needs Tata Neu three times for a reason the student would still have if the push, the coin, the price tick, the roommate, and the streak were all removed.

[INFERENCE] High-consideration Tata purchases remain a real but rare job: a phone or a laptop at Croma, a Westside outfit, a 1mg order, a trip. Zudio, the value fashion chain students are widely associated with in trade coverage, is store-only and is not on Neu. [RECOMMENDATION] That rare job can keep a resolver. It cannot be white space for a weekly open target. Calling the gap “a student super-app” restates the thesis Sivanandan said probably did not work.

## 7. Five concepts

These are five mechanisms. They are not five feeds, five games, or five wishlists. Each is destroyed in section 8. None is recommended.

### Hunger Door

**NAME.** Hunger Door.

**USER.** A college student living in a flat, PG, or hostel who already orders food several times a week.

**JOB.** Get the next meal when the mess loses, without planning a grocery shop.

**TRIGGER.** Hunger. Independent of Tata.

**FIRST OPEN.** The first unresolved meal of the week. The student opens a food surface and orders, or decides the mess is enough and does not order.

**SECOND OPEN.** The next hunger, later that day or on another day. Vaidya and Dhanith make a second food occasion plausible for this user.

**THIRD OPEN.** A further hunger. In the Pune sample, daily and multi-daily ordering exist, so a third food occasion can be real. It is still a meal, usually on Swiggy or Zomato.

**INTRINSIC VALUE.** The meal. No coin is required for the job to matter.

**TATA ADVANTAGE.** bbnow and Qmin are the only public Tata food-adjacent doors. They are not shown to be the student’s meal app.

**COMPETITOR.** Swiggy and Zomato. In the Pune sample those two names cover most stated preference.

**MOAT.** None against a food marketplace that already has the restaurants, the riders, and the habit.

**DATA LOOP.** Order history would teach the next meal. That loop belongs to the app that captured the order. Neu does not see Swiggy orders. Whether it sees bbnow orders placed in the BigBasket app is `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA`.

**BUSINESS VALUE.** A fulfilled Tata food order would be commerce. The evidenced orders are not Tata’s. A Neu tax on a Swiggy-shaped job adds cost and loses.

**MAIN RISK.** The team ships a food tab because the job is the most frequent thing in the research, and calls the Swiggy opens “Neu WAU.”

### Visible Rupee

**NAME.** Visible Rupee.

**USER.** A student who pays with UPI several times a week and is anxious about the money lasting until the next transfer.

**JOB.** See what is left for the rest of the week and decide the next small spend.

**TRIGGER.** A payment, or the arrival of money. In the Mangaluru sample the payment exists. The arrival cadence of parental money is `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`.

**FIRST OPEN.** Early in the week, to see the runway.

**SECOND OPEN.** After a spend that changes the runway.

**THIRD OPEN.** After another spend. This is the same screen again.

**INTRINSIC VALUE.** A true remaining-money figure would help a student on a fixed allowance. A partial figure would not.

**TATA ADVANTAGE.** The FY26 strategy is payments and loyalty. Neu can, in principle, show transactions it processed.

**COMPETITOR.** Google Pay and PhonePe, which already show the payments in the only student sample cited. The bank app shows the balance.

**MOAT.** A NeuCoin balance is not a view of the student’s money. The moat would require seeing non-Tata UPI. That visibility is not public.

**DATA LOOP.** Tata-processed payments only. For the dormant youth user, the loop is empty. Emptiness is `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA` as a rate, and it is the expected case given the Mangaluru preference.

**BUSINESS VALUE.** If students moved UPI onto Neu, payments monthly transacting users would move. The 10× aim needs a reason to switch rails. A worse or equal rail plus a coin is not that reason.

**MAIN RISK.** An empty or partial ledger is dressed with a streak so the open count moves.

### Third Class

**NAME.** Third Class.

**USER.** A student who already trains on a schedule and might book it.

**JOB.** Show up to the next session the student already intended.

**TRIGGER.** The student’s own plan. Not a Tata campaign.

**FIRST OPEN.** Book or check the first session of the week.

**SECOND OPEN.** The second session, if the plan has one.

**THIRD OPEN.** The third session, if the plan has one. The share of college students with three paid sessions in an ordinary week is `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`.

**INTRINSIC VALUE.** The session itself. A coin does not make the class useful.

**TATA ADVANTAGE.** Cult is on the partner-brand list. That is a brand relationship, not proof students book through Neu.

**COMPETITOR.** The Cult app, and the free campus gym, which needs no booking.

**MOAT.** None. Campus facilities are outside Tata. Cult’s habit, if any, sits with Cult.

**DATA LOOP.** Attendance at paid classes could predict the next booking. Neu only has that loop for students who already book through Tata. That base is `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA`.

**BUSINESS VALUE.** A Cult membership or class fee, for a subset, with an extra hop that suppresses conversion.

**MAIN RISK.** The third open is defined as “we reminded them,” which the brief forbids, because the median student has no third class.

### Broken Kit

**NAME.** Broken Kit.

**USER.** A student whose charger, earphones, laptop, medicine, or similar kit has actually failed or run out.

**JOB.** Replace the broken thing with a product Tata can fulfil, at a known rupee outcome.

**TRIGGER.** The break. Episodic.

**FIRST OPEN.** The break, or the decision to replace it. This open is strong because the pain is specific.

**SECOND OPEN.** A second look in the same episode: a store, a price, a 1mg substitute. Plausible inside that episode.

**THIRD OPEN.** A third look in the same week only if new information appears. A price change is rejected as the reason. If nothing new is true, the third open is a refresh.

**INTRINSIC VALUE.** The replacement. NeuCoins matter only as rupees off an eligible purchase, at 1 NeuCoin = ₹1 where the brand allows it. Air India-style exclusions show the rupee is not universal.

**TATA ADVANTAGE.** Croma, Westside, and 1mg can fulfil some of these episodes. Zudio cannot, because it is not on Neu. This is the strongest Tata right-to-win in the set, and it is still an event.

**COMPETITOR.** Amazon, the brand’s own store, a campus shop, and the Croma or 1mg app itself.

**MOAT.** A true in-store rupee and a nearby Croma, during a real purchase. Copyable as a price check. Not a weekly moat.

**DATA LOOP.** The episode ends when the item is replaced or abandoned. The next loop may be months away. Cycle length is `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`.

**BUSINESS VALUE.** Real GMV when the episode is real. No contribution to ordinary-week WAU.

**MAIN RISK.** The team keeps the kit on a permanent list so the app has something to ping about. That is NeuRoom again.

### Parent Ping

**NAME.** Parent Ping.

**USER.** A student who receives money from a parent and spends it on campus.

**JOB.** Receive a purposed transfer and use it.

**TRIGGER.** The parent sends money.

**FIRST OPEN.** The money arrives. That open has a clear reason.

**SECOND OPEN.** The student pays it out, or the parent sends again. The second move is often the other person, or a payment that Google Pay already does.

**THIRD OPEN.** There is no independent third reason in an ordinary week. A third open appears only if the parent acts again, or if the student is nudged to “check the allowance.”

**INTRINSIC VALUE.** Receiving money is real. Splitting it into envelopes is a finance preference, not a proven weekly job. Allowance frequency is `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`.

**TATA ADVANTAGE.** Payments are the stated FY26 bet. A purposed transfer could sit next to bill pay.

**COMPETITOR.** Google Pay and PhonePe family transfers. The parent’s bank app.

**MOAT.** None, unless the purpose code unlocks a Tata good the student was going to buy anyway. That is a purchase event, not a third open.

**DATA LOOP.** Transfer history. It does not reveal college or roommates. It reveals that a parent paid.

**BUSINESS VALUE.** Float and a payments user, if both sides adopt Neu. Two-sided adoption is `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`.

**MAIN RISK.** This is NeuSlip with the roommate swapped for a parent. Open 2 waits on another person. The concept gets a coin for “opening the allowance” when the parent is slow.

## 8. Brutal rejection analysis

The gate is five answers, all required. A notification, a reward, a price change, another person acting, or a streak cannot be the third session. The session needs its own user-value reason, and that reason has to be a reason to open Tata Neu.

**Hunger Door.** Why open: hunger. Why again: the next hunger. Why a third time: a further hunger, which the away-from-home food samples make plausible, and which does not need a push. Why Tata Neu rather than another app: it cannot answer. Swiggy and Zomato already finish the meal, and the Pune sample says so. Why Tata benefits: it does not, unless the order moves to a Tata surface students have not been shown to prefer. [RECOMMENDATION] Reject. The best third session in the set is somebody else’s session. Building it inside Neu is the clone the CEO’s interview already warned against. It also fails the case’s “without a purchase” test, because the value of the session is the order.

**Visible Rupee.** Why open: to see if the week’s money will last. Why again: only if a new transaction changed the figure. Why a third time: the same check. The payments that would change the figure sit in Google Pay in the cited sample, and Neu does not see them. A third open would be a refresh of a partial number, or a prompt. Why Tata Neu: the FY26 payments strategy is Tata’s reason, not the student’s. The student already has the ledger in the app that took the payment. Why Tata benefits: only if UPI volume moves. Nothing public says students will move it. [RECOMMENDATION] Reject. A ledger that cannot see the week is not a product. Coins on Neu UPI would be paying for the open.

**Third Class.** Why open: a class the student already meant to attend. Why again: the next class on a plan that has not been evidenced for the median student. Why a third time: a third class. That frequency is `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`. For everyone else the third open is a reminder, which is forbidden. Why Tata Neu: Cult’s app or the campus gym is the shorter path. Why Tata benefits: a class fee from a small, unmeasured base, after an extra hop. [RECOMMENDATION] Reject. Right-to-win is a logo on a card partner list, not a student habit.

**Broken Kit.** Why open: something broke. Why again: to finish that same decision, which can be a real second session inside one episode. Why a third time: it cannot answer for an ordinary week. The third look needs new information. Price and stock ticks are banned as the reason. In a week where nothing broke, there is no open. Why Tata Neu: Croma, 1mg, or Westside can be the right door during the episode, and during that episode this is the least fake Tata story in the set. Why Tata benefits: GMV on a real replacement. [RECOMMENDATION] Reject as the weekly product. It is NeuRoom with the roommate removed. Keep the resolver instinct for the day something actually breaks. Do not staff a squad to make the kit recur.

**Parent Ping.** Why open: money arrived. Why again: the parent, or a payout the student can do in Google Pay. Why a third time: it cannot answer. Why Tata Neu: it cannot answer against the rail the parent and the student already share. Why Tata benefits: a payments user, if both sides move, which is untested. [RECOMMENDATION] Reject. Another person’s action is the engine. That is the NeuSlip failure with a different cast.

[INFERENCE] No hybrid repairs this. Hunger plus a rupee ledger is Swiggy plus Google Pay. A kit plus a parent is an event plus another person. A class plus a coin is Cult plus a reward. The case’s metric is youth weekly active users of Tata Neu, up 15 percent versus control, from meaningful opens, with open-to-shop as a bonus. The jobs that could move a weekly count do not open Neu. The jobs that can open Neu do not happen weekly.

[HYPOTHESIS] It remains possible that Tata’s own logs already show some youth cohort opening Neu two or three times a week without a purchase. If that is true, this brief is the wrong problem. That baseline is `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA`. [RECOMMENDATION] Read the logs before anyone writes another concept. Do not invent the baseline in order to justify a build.

## 10. Winning concept

**None.**

[RECOMMENDATION] Do not pick Hunger Door because it won the table. The gate in section 8 rejects it. Do not pick Broken Kit because it is the most Tata. The ordinary week rejects it. The recommended output is a non-build plus a short research program: a student diary and a Tata log pull, aimed at killing the weekly-open brief if the diary confirms what the public evidence already suggests.

There is no product name. Giving the research a Neu-prefixed brand would invite a build.

## 11. Why it is fundamentally different

[INFERENCE] Nothing here is a new mechanism that escapes the previous two failures. NeuRoom failed because the object was a purchase event. NeuSlip failed because the second open was another person and the third open was not promised. The five concepts above are the remaining shapes a team reaches for: the frequent job Tata does not own, the ledger Tata cannot see, the body schedule Tata does not host, the rare job Tata does own, and the parent as a substitute for the roommate.

[INFERENCE] The structural fact is the same in all five. A third meaningful open of Tata Neu, in an ordinary week, without a purchase, requires an object the student needs three times, that Tata can finish, that a faster app does not already finish, and that still matters if nobody is nudged. Public evidence supplies objects that fail at least one of those tests every time. [RECOMMENDATION] Stop generating concepts until a diary produces an object that passes all four tests in the student’s own week.

## 12. Product definition

[RECOMMENDATION] Non-build. No feature, no tab, no cohort, no campus pilot of a product.

The research program has two questions and no interface.

First question, for Tata analytics: among users Tata is willing to call youth, how many open Neu at least three times in an ordinary week, and how many of those sessions contain no order and no payment? Baseline is `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA`. If the company cannot define youth without inventing a college flag, record that as the finding. Neu does not publicly know college, hostel, or roommates.

Second question, for students recruited outside Neu: in the next fourteen days, what did you open, why, and did any Tata brand appear? The diary is the product definition. A concept may be written only if a job shows up on three separate days, in the student’s words, with a reason that is not a push, a reward, a price change, another person, or a streak, and an existing Tata service can finish that job without becoming Swiggy, Google Pay, Instagram, WhatsApp, or Amazon.

## 13. First open

[RECOMMENDATION] There is no product first open.

For the research, the first open is methodological. Analytics opens the youth definition and reports the distribution of weekly sessions, including zeros. In parallel, a student starts a diary on a teaching week, not an exam week and not a festival week. The diary asks what they did and which app they used. It does not ask “would you open Tata Neu.”

[INFERENCE] Hunger Door’s first open is real and belongs to a food app. Visible Rupee’s first open is real only if the number on screen is the student’s actual money. Third Class’s first open is real only for someone who already has a class. Broken Kit’s first open is real only when something broke. Parent Ping’s first open is real only when money arrived. None of those is a reason to ship a Neu session.

## 14. Second open

[RECOMMENDATION] There is no product second open to design.

The diary’s second day is successful if the student records another real job, including “nothing I needed an app for.” A blank day is data. Paying the student per open, or prompting them to open Neu, invalidates the day.

[INFERENCE] The second sessions that public evidence already supports are another meal, another UPI payment in Google Pay, and another WhatsApp thread. A second Neu session is `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA`. Until that distribution is pulled, a roadmap that assumes a second open is an assumption, not a plan.

## 15. Third open

[RECOMMENDATION] There is no product third open.

This is the session the last two concepts could not earn. The research counts a third occasion only when the student describes a separate reason, on a separate day or a clearly separate moment, and would have done it had Tata sent nothing. A third food order counts as a third food occasion. It counts as a third Neu open only if the student used Neu. Expect that intersection to be empty until the logs say otherwise. The expected rate is `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH` and `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA`. [HYPOTHESIS] The intersection is near zero for ordinary weeks. That hypothesis is the kill test, not a claim that it has been measured.

## 16. Habit loop

[INFERENCE] The habit loops that exist are already closed.

Hunger returns, and Swiggy or Zomato or the mess closes it. A small payment returns, and Google Pay closes it in the cited sample. The circle returns, and WhatsApp closes it. The timetable returns, and the college closes it. Tata’s loop, on public evidence, is spend, then a NeuCoin where the brand allows it, then silence until the next spend. [EVIDENCE] The FY26 report points Neu at financial services and loyalty so that recognised spend leads to a card, a payment, or an investment. That flywheel starts at a transaction. It does not start at a non-purchase open.

[RECOMMENDATION] Do not draw a loop diagram for a weekly Neu habit. A loop that needs a manufactured trigger is the streak, the scratch card, and the daily check-in this brief bans.

## 17. Tata moat

The moat is not “many brands.”

[INFERENCE] What Tata actually has, when a purchase is already underway, is a rupee-denominated loyalty unit on eligible brands, a co-branded card, a large Croma store network beyond a pure marketplace, a grocery business, and a pharmacy business. [EVIDENCE] 1 NeuCoin equals ₹1 where redemption is allowed. Air India redemption is excluded in the HDFC Tata Neu terms. Zudio is not on Neu, so the fashion brand closest to a student budget is outside the moat. Westside is inside it, and student frequency on Westside is unmeasured.

[INFERENCE] None of those assets compels a non-purchase open. They shorten a purchase the student has already started, and only on brands that honour the rupee. A moat that appears at checkout cannot be the reason the app was opened on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday of a week with no checkout. [RECOMMENDATION] Use the moat inside the brand apps and the stores, which is the complement strategy the CEO stated. Do not spend it on a student layer that has no weekly object.

## 18. Data advantage

[INFERENCE] Tata’s data advantage on this brief is smaller than the ecosystem story suggests.

Neu can know Tata transactions it processed, NeuCoin balances, and card spends the bank relationship allows it to see. The exact join across Croma, BigBasket, 1mg, Westside, and Neu UPI for one youth user is `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA`. Neu does not publicly know college, hostel, or roommates. It does not see Google Pay, PhonePe, Swiggy, Zomato, WhatsApp, or the mess biometric. [INFERENCE] The interesting student week is exactly the week those other systems see and Tata does not.

[RECOMMENDATION] The only data advantage worth using in the next month is honesty about the denominator. Pull youth session counts, including users with zero sessions. If “youth” is a self-reported age bucket, label it that way. Do not infer a hostel from a pin code. Do not buy a campus graph in order to manufacture a segment the diary has not justified.

## 19. Business model

[RECOMMENDATION] No new revenue model. No coins for opening. No student subscription for a weekly surface. No take rate on a meal Tata does not fulfil.

[EVIDENCE] The business already loses money at group-digital scale: FY26 loss ₹4,974 crore on ₹46,515 crore GMV, after an FY25 loss of ₹828 crore. The stated model is loyalty and financial services attached to spend: payments, card, lending, insurance. [INFERENCE] That model pays when a customer spends on an eligible Tata or partner surface. It does not pay for empty opens. Open-to-shop can stay a bonus metric in an experiment that has a real shop. It cannot be the excuse for a habit feature with no shop in the ordinary week.

[RECOMMENDATION] If a later diary found a true weekly Tata job, the business model would still be the existing one: fulfil the job in the brand that already can, and let Neu recognise the spend. Until that job exists, the profitable act is not building.

## 20. L0/L1/L2 metrics

No baselines are invented. Every current value below is unknown.

**L0.** Youth weekly active users of Tata Neu, treatment versus control, with the case’s bar at +15 percent. Current youth WAU is `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA`. The definition of youth is `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA`. Without that denominator the +15 percent bar cannot be sized, powered, or defended.

**L1.** Meaningful opens per youth user per ordinary week. A meaningful open is a session the user started for a reason they can state, which still exists if the push, the coin, the price tick, the other person, and the streak are removed, and which is not only a purchase confirmation. Current distribution is `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA`. The diary’s parallel distribution is `TBD — REQUIRES PRIMARY RESEARCH`.

**L2.** Open-to-shop: the share of those meaningful opens that reach an eligible Tata order or an eligible in-store redemption within the same episode. Current rate is `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA`. This is the bonus in the case. It is not a substitute for L0.

[RECOMMENDATION] Report medians and the share at zero. An average inflated by exam-week buyers will recreate NeuRoom’s error. Do not set a target open count until L0 and L1 exist. A target written before the baseline is a wish.

## 21. Experiment

[RECOMMENDATION] Do not A/B a feature. There is no feature.

The experiment is a two-part measurement with a pre-registered kill, described in section 22.

Part A is internal. Pull eight ordinary weeks, excluding a pre-declared exam and festival window if the academic calendar of the user’s city is known, otherwise exclude nothing and tag the weeks. For the youth definition Tata actually has, report users, sessions per week, sessions with no order and no payment, and repeat rate. If the definition is “age on the profile” or “cannot be defined,” say so in the first line of the readout.

Part B is external. A fourteen-day diary with college students who are not recruited from Tata Neu’s user base, split by living with parents versus living in a flat, PG, or hostel. Cities can include the campus cities already discussed in prior work, but the diary is not a launch. The instrument records the job, the app, and whether a Tata brand was involved. It does not include a prototype, a concierge placing orders, or a Neu login.

[HYPOTHESIS] Part A will show event-driven youth use, consistent with the case. Part B will show meals, payments, chat, and classes, consistent with sections 3 and 4. Both hypotheses are untested in this note. Failing to confirm them is a reason to reopen the question. Confirming them is a reason to stop.

Sample size for a WAU experiment is `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA`, because power depends on the baseline and the variance. Do not publish a fake n. The diary in the 30-day plan is a learning sample, not a powered estimate of +15 percent.

## 22. Kill criteria

[RECOMMENDATION] Kill the weekly-open program, and do not write a sixth concept, if any one of these is true.

1. Part A cannot define youth, or defines it and shows that three non-purchase opens in an ordinary week are rare. “Rare” means the business would have to manufacture the behaviour to approach the case bar. The numeric cut is set only after the baseline exists. It is `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA` until then. The decision rule today is qualitative and binding: if the mass of the distribution sits at zero or one session, stop.
2. Part B’s diaries show the recurring jobs are class, food, UPI, and chat, and the apps are the college, Swiggy or Zomato, Google Pay or PhonePe, and WhatsApp or Instagram.
3. The only Tata appearances in the diary are purchases or purchase research, which is the NeuRoom event.
4. The only way to force a third session in a design critique is a push, a coin, a price change, another person, or a streak.
5. Fulfilment requires a new marketplace, a new social graph, or a new payments rail that replaces Google Pay.
6. A team proposes coins, streaks, scratch cards, daily check-ins, fake scarcity, trivia, generic deals, or a recommendation tray as the fix for a failed third open.

[RECOMMENDATION] Also kill the research early if Part A shows the case is already satisfied and youth already open Neu two or three times a week for a meaningful non-purchase reason. In that world the brief is false and a feature is still the wrong response. Write that down and stop.

## 23. MVP

[RECOMMENDATION] Scope is research only. No screens, no design file, no prototype, no campus brand campaign.

In scope: a one-page analytics spec for Part A; a diary instrument of about ten items per day covering sleep location, whether it was a teaching day, meals and where they came from, payments and which app, academic work, any Tata brand touched, and the reason for each app session the student considers deliberate; recruitment outside Neu; a codebook; a readout that is allowed to say “do not build.”

Out of scope: Neu UX, copy, coins, push templates, roommate invites, college single sign-on, price alerts, a food tab, a wallet redesign, a Cult deep link, and any “student mode.”

The diary n for this learning pass is a design choice, not a finding. [RECOMMENDATION] Complete 40 diaries, 20 living with parents and 20 living away, across more than one city, over 14 days that are not an exam week. Forty diaries cannot estimate a national WAU effect. They can show whether a third independent Tata reason appears at all. If recruitment cannot reach 40, report the shortfall. Do not fill the gap with Neu employees.

## 24. Risks

[INFERENCE] The largest risk is shipping Hunger Door because 50.8 beat 36.4. The score measured a real job in the wrong app.

[INFERENCE] The next risk is a loyalty program that pays students to open. It will move L0 for as long as the payment continues and will teach the wrong lesson. It is banned in section 22.

[INFERENCE] The third risk is partnership theatre. A college MoU that imports a student ID does not create a weekly job. It creates a segment label. Labels are not opens.

[INFERENCE] The fourth risk is using Bain, BCG, or the food-app samples as if they were Tata Neu frequencies. They are shopping, research, and meals.

[INFERENCE] The fifth risk is the P&L. Another year of engagement features on a business that lost ₹4,974 crore in FY26, while the stated strategy is payments and loyalty, is a strategy contradiction. Qualitative Neu UPI complaints make a payments push at students a quality problem as well as a preference problem. The failure rate remains `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA`.

[HYPOTHESIS] A diary can still surprise the team. Students might already use bbnow or 1mg on three separate days for a reason Neu could host without a new marketplace. If the diaries show that, the non-build is wrong and a narrow test can be written. Until they show that, the surprise is a hope.

## 25. 30-day validation plan

[RECOMMENDATION] Thirty days, one question: does any ordinary college week contain three independent reasons to open Tata Neu? The plan validates the non-build. It does not validate a concept.

**Days 1–5.** Freeze the youth definition with analytics. Pull the eight-week distribution in section 21. If the pull is blocked, the day-5 note says the baseline is `TBD — REQUIRES TATA INTERNAL DATA` and names the blocker. Do not substitute a public number.

**Days 1–5, in parallel.** Freeze the diary. Pre-register the kill rules in section 22. Recruit only off Neu. Exclude the exam window using the student’s own college calendar, which they can name even if Tata cannot.

**Days 6–20.** Field the 14-day diary. Do not show Tata concepts. Do not help participants order, pay, or split. A researcher who answers “you could use Neu for that” has contaminated the day.

**Days 21–24.** Code jobs from the student’s words. Allowed codes start from section 3 and may add a job the student actually named. A code may not be “engagement” or “discovery.”

**Days 25–27.** Join the diary to the question, not to a solution. Count students with three separate moments whose reason survives the ban on pushes, coins, price ticks, other people, and streaks, and whose app was Tata Neu or could have been an existing Tata service without a new marketplace.

**Days 28–30.** Write a kill memo or a one-job exception. The exception may name one job. It may not attach a roadmap, a screen, or a second job “while we are here.” If the count in days 25–27 is effectively none, the memo’s first sentence is the first paragraph of this note: no concept can credibly create the opens.

[RECOMMENDATION] No success theatre. A completed diary with zero Tata moments is a successful validation of the non-build.

## 26. Final panel attack

A panel that still wants a feature should have to answer these, in this order, with evidence rather than a prototype.

1. Name the ordinary-week object the student needs on three separate occasions. If the object is “a Tata product they might buy,” that is NeuRoom. Sit down.
2. Show the third occasion in a diary, not in a storyboard. If the third occasion is a roommate, a parent, a price change, a push, a coin, or a streak, it is disqualified.
3. Say which app finishes that job today. If the answer is Swiggy, Zomato, Google Pay, PhonePe, WhatsApp, Instagram, YouTube, or the college, the feature is a clone or a hop.
4. Explain why the student opens Tata Neu rather than that app, given that Indians already spend more than 70 percent of their time in five apps and the CEO has told Neu to stop competing for those visits.
5. Explain the Tata P&L. Empty opens do not pay down a ₹4,974 crore loss. Open-to-shop on a job Tata does not fulfil is a chart, not a business.
6. State the current youth WAU. If the answer is a target rather than a measured baseline, the experiment is not ready.
7. Say what happens in a week where the student is fine: fed, paid, in class, and nothing is broken. If the honest answer is “Neu stays closed,” the product does not meet the brief.

[RECOMMENDATION] A concept that fails any one of these is not “worth a small test.” NeuRoom and NeuSlip were small tests on paper. They failed the third open before a line of product was written. Another small test of the same hole wastes the month the diary needs.

## 27. Final recommendation

[RECOMMENDATION] Do not build. Do not rename NeuSlip. Do not revive NeuRoom. Do not fund Hunger Door, Visible Rupee, Third Class, Broken Kit, or Parent Ping.

[RECOMMENDATION] Tell the case sponsors that two or three meaningful Tata Neu opens a week, without a purchase, is not a credible product outcome on the evidence in hand. The jobs that recur belong to the mess, the timetable, Swiggy, Zomato, Google Pay, and WhatsApp. Tata’s right to show up is a real purchase on Croma, Westside, BigBasket, 1mg, or an eligible payment, and that purchase is an event. The FY26 strategy already points Neu at loyalty and financial services attached to spend. A youth engagement feature fights that strategy and the CEO’s instruction to complement brand apps.

[RECOMMENDATION] Spend the next 30 days on the log pull and the 40 diaries. If they reproduce this note, close the brief. If they surface one job that passes section 22, write one narrow test for that job and nothing else. Until then, the winning concept is none.
