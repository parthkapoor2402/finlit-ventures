# Storyboard

The deck argues that Skip Check should be tested, and it stops before any wireframe.

1. Business context. Contributions rose from the July 2026 AMFI note to the August note. The chart shows July’s new registrations beside discontinued or tenure-completed SIPs, and the sentence under it says that shorter bar is not a count of voluntary pauses. FinLit was founded publicly in 2026 by Nishant Jain. Skip Check is free. ₹99 is an unpaid list price. The prototype does not take payment or book a review. Discretionary-management wording on the same site is a compliance tension, not a feature. The job is reconstructed, not an interview. The actors are the person, their app, and FinLit.

2. User evidence. Three Reddit threads are paraphrased and linked. Under them, Kuvera, Groww, and Mint with Economic Times are each read as evidence, then an interpretation marked as a hypothesis, then an implication. Nobody has been interviewed.

3. One person, two months. Ananya Shah is a teaching composite from the research notes. The market-worry month and the cash-gap month are the same person. Pause is a legitimate result in the second month. The four outcomes are intended, and none is measured. SEBI’s survey is linked as background, not as her result.

4. Journey and hypotheses. The live order is the assessment: two landing doors, an optional reason that changes emphasis and not the formula, amounts, equal Keep / Reduce / Pause, What if, optional questions, a local recap, and a local reminder. Pause is a proposed ₹0. The bot explains and does not decide. Analytics stay in memory and store no amounts. The chart shows the four desk means. H2 is selected because it still helps if most pauses are legitimate. H2 is falsified if a cash-need user feels pressed to continue or cannot restate the arithmetic. research/17 still says the prototype was not built. docs/product-decisions.md describes the earlier card. informedChoiceRate belongs to that earlier event list and has no observed rate.

5. Problem framing. The canvas is the true problem, who, how we know, and value that is only potential. Why look now is a hypothesis, because contributions are still rising. The 38.5 crore TAM and “NCFE 2026” are rejected. The 27% literacy figure is 2019 fieldwork. Customer value and business value are both unmeasured.

6. What to validate next. The order is counsel, then usability, then distribution. None has been run. What we believe is separated from what is unproven, including the RICE reach of 200. The prototype address is linked and is not usage evidence. The deck stops before wireframes.
