# Storyboard

The deck argues that Skip Check should be tested, and it stops before any wireframe.

1. Business context. Contributions rose from the July 2026 AMFI note to the August note. The chart shows July’s new registrations beside discontinued or tenure-completed SIPs, and the sentence under it says that shorter bar is not a count of voluntary pauses. Skip Check is free. ₹99 is a list price. The prototype does not take payment, and it does not change the SIP.

2. User evidence. Three Reddit threads are paraphrased and linked. Kuvera, Groww, Mint, and Economic Times are linked underneath. Each item is labeled as one anecdote or as commentary. Nobody has been interviewed.

3. One person, two months. Ananya Shah is a teaching composite from the research notes. The market-worry month and the cash-gap month are the same person. Pause is a legitimate result in the second month. The four outcomes are intended, and none is measured. SEBI’s survey is linked as background, not as her result.

4. Journey and hypotheses. The live order is landing, optional reason, amounts, Keep / Reduce / Pause, What if, optional questions, and a local recap. The chart shows the four desk means. H2 is selected. H2 is falsified if a cash-need user feels pressed to continue or cannot restate the arithmetic. The live page does not compute the older informed-choice rate.

5. Problem framing. The problem is the explanation at the moment of change. The unknown is the voluntary share of July’s 50.30 lakh. Why look now is a hypothesis, because contributions are still rising. Customer value and business value are both potential.

6. What to validate next. Usability, distribution, and counsel are proposed tests. The reach figure of 200 is an assumption. The prototype address is linked and is not usage evidence. The deck ends there.
