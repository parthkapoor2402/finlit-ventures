# Skip Check — storyboard

Six pages. 16:9. Conclusion headline on every page. No wireframes and no pages after 06. The generator is `build.mjs`.

The reader should leave believing three things: the SIP book is not in crisis, the published stoppage count is the wrong object, and Skip Check is a test of a reason-aware arithmetic check that has not been run on users.

## 01 — Business context

**Headline.** Contributions are still rising. The published stoppage count is not a count of voluntary pauses.

**Job of the page.** Put FinLit and Skip Check in a market that is growing, and take the stoppage ratio off the table as a size of prize.

**Blocks, in reading order.**

1. Three stats, unequal on purpose. Contribution and account count establish that the book is rising. The wider third stat is the conclusion: 81.87% is discontinued or tenure-completed versus new, and AMFI’s ceased-SIP rule includes three failed instalments.
2. Where FinLit stands: 2026 public founding, not registered with SEBI or AMFI, discretionary-management wording as a compliance tension, free Skip Check versus an unpaid ₹99 list price, honest not-connected panel, prototype URL as a deploy check only.
3. Reconstructed job sentence, labeled as not locked and not an interview. Four actors: the person, their app, FinLit, and the evidence limit.

**Do not say.** That stops are exploding. That 10.02 crore accounts are 10.02 crore people. That anyone has used the prototype. That FinLit is registered. That ₹99 has converted.

## 02 — Evidence

**Headline.** Desk research and public threads. Nobody has been interviewed.

**Job of the page.** Show the whole evidence base at the altitude an executive can check, and label every leap.

**Blocks.**

1. Three paraphrased anecdotes, one line each, with the community name and no username.
2. A three-column reading: Evidence, Interpretation, Implication. Rows are H4, H1 with H2, and the help-centre confusion. Means sit on the row so the scores are not a separate unsupported chart.
3. Footer points at Mint and Economic Times as commentary.

**Do not say.** A prevalence. A quote. That “never stop” is a differentiated product. That the voluntary share of the 50.30 lakh is known.

## 03 — Persona

**Headline.** One illustrative person, two situations. Ananya has not been interviewed.

**Job of the page.** Make the cash branch the same woman as the market-worry month, and separate intended outcomes from metrics that exist.

**Blocks.**

1. One identity bar. Age, city, salary, two SIPs, ₹12,000, house goal. Teaching case. Public-thread anchor. No second name.
2. Two situations side by side, labeled as the same person.
3. Outcome matrix: immediate, behavioural, trust, business. The header says none of this is measured.
4. Split strip: what the prototype actually records, versus the usability and distribution gates, which have not been run.

**Do not say.** That Ananya was interviewed. That her SIP size is a market average. That `informedChoiceRate` has a value. A second persona.

## 04 — Journey and hypotheses

**Headline.** The breakdown is the reason and the numbers. H2 is selected, and it has not been tested.

**Job of the page.** Tie the live assessment to H2, show why H2 survives if pauses are legitimate, and put the metric discrepancy where it cannot be missed.

**Blocks.**

1. The same reconstructed job, once, so the journey has a sentence to serve.
2. Eight steps of `AssessmentApp`. Reason and amounts are marked. The formula sits under the list. Keep, Reduce, and Pause are equal. The bot explains and does not decide.
3. H1–H4 with means. H2 is the only filled row. A line under H4 stops a reader from treating 2.8 as “the warning is weak.”
4. Why H2, and the falsifier, marked proposed and not run.
5. A full-width discrepancy: `research/17` still says the prototype was not built; `docs/product-decisions.md` describes the earlier card; `informedChoiceRate` is that earlier event set; the assessment emits different events; neither has an observed rate.

**Do not say.** That the reason changes the formula. That a restart month is in the live journey. That the rate was measured. That RICE selected H2.

## 05 — Problem framing

**Headline.** The problem is an under-specified confirm, not a crisis in the SIP book.

**Job of the page.** Answer the five canvas questions without turning them into benefit cards, then close on one sentence.

**Blocks.**

1. True problem.
2. Who, with the SEBI household figures and the awareness/participation split.
3. How we know, including the 36% knowledge figure as background.
4. Customer value and business value, labeled potential. Explicit rejection of the 38.5 crore TAM and of “NCFE 2026.” The 27% figure is dated 2019.
5. Why now, labeled a hypothesis. The July ratio fell.
6. Synthesis band: explain the next instalments in light of the person’s reason, keep the three choices equal, and do not size the problem with the stoppage ratio.

**Do not say.** A crisis. A measured willingness to pay. That 63% awareness is the market.

## 06 — Synthesis and validation

**Headline.** Test the mechanism. Do not report a result that has not been measured.

**Job of the page.** Separate belief from proof, name the three gates in order, and stop before screens.

**Blocks.**

1. What we believe. Five lines, all either the selected hypothesis or a property of the calculator.
2. What is unproven. Interviews, restatement, pressure, any rate, and RICE reach of 200 as an assumption.
3. Three gates in order: counsel, usability (7 of 10, pause without hunting), distribution (10 of the first 50). Each is marked not run. Continuation is not the distribution gate.
4. Close, on forest: this deck stops before wireframes. A later section would show screens. Those pages are not included.

**Do not say.** A ship date. A pass. A wireframe. A partner embed. That counsel has been completed.

## Thread across the six

01 removes the false market. 02 shows how thin the human evidence is. 03 refuses a second persona and refuses to treat intentions as results. 04 locates the break in the live product and parks the old metric. 05 states the problem at canvas scale. 06 asks for three tests and ends.
