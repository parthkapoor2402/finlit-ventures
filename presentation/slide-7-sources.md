# Slide 7 sources

The formula is the one in `src/domain/sip-calculator.ts`: contribution difference = (current monthly SIP − proposed monthly SIP) × months, in whole rupees, for 1 to 24 months. An increase is rejected. Pause uses a proposed amount of ₹0. An amount above ₹10,00,000 asks the user to confirm the digits.

The worked example is illustrative, using the same inputs as slide 6: ₹12,000, ₹3,500, and 6 months. Keep plans ₹72,000. Reduce plans ₹21,000, which is ₹51,000 less. Pause plans ₹0, which is ₹72,000 less. The selected reason does not change this arithmetic.

Informed Choice Rate is a proposed definition for sessions that reach a valid comparison. The figure 35 ÷ 50 = 70% is an illustrative calculation. It is not observed product data. Keep, Reduce, and Pause remain neutral outcomes.

The usability sample of 8 to 10 people, the 7-of-10 restatement bar, and the distribution check on the first 50 non-staff sessions are planned gates from the research notes. They have not been run.

FinLit states that it is not registered with SEBI or AMFI. A compliance review of this prototype has not been completed. A written compliance review is required before any live pilot.
