# Skip Check deck — design system

Six slides, 16:9. The editable file is `out/finlit-skip-check-strategy.pptx`, generated by `build.mjs` (PptxGenJS 4.0.1). This note is the contract for that file. It adapts patterns from the reference decks and does not copy their brands, market claims, or palettes.

## Canvas

| Token | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Slide | 13.333 in × 7.5 in |
| Margin | 0.42 in |
| Content width | 12.493 in |
| Footer rule | y = 7.06 in |
| Footer type | 10 pt, bottom of the margin band |
| Text box margin | 0 in every `addText` call |

Whitespace is part of the grid. A region is either a claim, a source, or empty on purpose. There is no decorative illustration.

## Colour

Skip Check tokens only. Reference-deck navy, pink, and sage are not used.

| Role | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paper | `#F6F1E8` | Slide ground |
| Ink | `#1C1917` | Body and headlines |
| Forest | `#1E4D3A` | Top rule, selected hypothesis, synthesis bands, key numbers |
| Card | `#FFFDF8` | Panels |
| Line | `#E4DCD0` | Rules and panel strokes |
| Amber | `#8A5A2B` | Short labels only: source status, “not run”, section kickers |
| Muted | `#44403C` | Secondary sentences and footers |

Amber is not used for paragraphs. Forest on paper, and paper on forest, carry the emphasis. One wash, `#E7F0EB`, marks the two journey steps where the product breaks. One warm wash, `#F3E6D4`, marks the metric discrepancy. Neither is a new brand colour; both are tints of the tokens above.

## Type

| Role | Face | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Headline | Georgia | 24 pt, one or two lines, conclusion first |
| Panel title | Georgia | 14–16 pt |
| Job sentence | Georgia | 14 pt |
| Body | Calibri | 12–14 pt |
| Table and compact rows | Calibri | 11–12 pt |
| Labels | Calibri | 10–11 pt, often tracked |
| Footer sources | Calibri | 10 pt |
| Page index | Georgia | 12–13 pt |

Currency figures are Calibri, including the large stat on slide 01. The rupee sign is required, and the Georgia cut used for QA does not contain U+20B9. Headlines stay in Georgia and do not carry a rupee sign. No emoji. No text autofit, so type is not silently shrunk.

## What was adapted

- BITS AND PIECES: a numbered section index, a research reading order, and a five-question problem canvas. Not their market-size claims, and not their dense small type.
- DOC: a large serif headline and a light interior after a quiet open. The open here stays on paper, with a forest rule, rather than a copied dark cover.
- Myntra: conclusion-led headlines, a source footer, and a comparison matrix for outcomes and for believe / unproven. Not their brand colours, and not a full-height brand rail.
- NL: numbered markers, a big serif line, and a two-column comparison. Not decorative emptiness, and not an unverified statistic used as decoration.

## What a slide is allowed to do

One dominant headline. The headline is the conclusion. Supporting blocks are unequal when their jobs are unequal: on slide 01 the stoppage stat is wider than the two contribution stats; on slide 05 the value question is wider than “why now”; on slide 06 the close is a single sentence, not a third column of benefits.

Parallel items that are genuinely parallel — three anecdotes, four hypotheses, three gates, five canvas questions — are aligned. They are not icon cards with equal slogans.

```bash
cd presentation

pdftoppm -png -r 120 out/finlit-skip-check-strategy.pdf /tmp/skip-slides/slide
```
