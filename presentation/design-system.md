# Skip Check strategy deck

Six pages, 13.333 by 7.5 inches. The file names Georgia for headlines and Calibri for body text, so PowerPoint on a normal Office install has the faces. The PDF check on this machine substitutes Gelasio for Georgia and Carlito for Calibri, which are the metric matches installed here.

## Type

Headlines are Georgia at 26 pt. Card titles are Georgia at 15 or 16 pt. Body is Calibri at 14 pt. Source lines and the footer are Calibri at 11 pt or larger. Text boxes use a zero margin so a line is not clipped by the default inset. Section labels are written as words, not as compressed capitals.

## Charts

Two charts, and only two, because only two series are sourced.

July 2026 activity is a pair of horizontal bars. The label sits on its own line above the bar. The value sits in a column to the right of the track, not on the fill. The scale is 0 to 70 lakh. New registrations are 61.44. Discontinued or tenure-completed SIPs are 50.30.

Hypothesis means are horizontal bars on a 0 to 5 scale: H1 3.7, H2 4.3, H3 3.5, H4 2.8. H2 is the darker bar because it is the explanation selected for a test. The caption says that in a sentence. The scores are equal-weight desk scores, not a survey.

## Links

A source name is underlined in forest green and carries the full URL. The visible text is the publication, not a raw address, except the three Reddit paths and `skip-check.vercel.app`, which are short enough to read.
