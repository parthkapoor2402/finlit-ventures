# Slide 8 sources

Informed Choice Rate is a proposed North Star for a future pilot. It is not a measured result on the live page. The numerator is eligible sessions that record Keep, Reduce, or Pause, restate the contribution difference, and recognise that a pause does not sell existing units. The denominator is eligible sessions that reach a valid comparison card.

Every percentage on the slide is a proposed pilot threshold or a hypothesis. The ≥20% pre-confirm figure is a discovery hypothesis. Invalid-input recovery has no numeric target until a baseline exists. Decision-path mix has no preferred distribution.

The 8–10 person study is formative usability testing. It is not statistically representative, and it is not proof of demand. Automated tests are the check that displayed differences match the formula in `src/domain/sip-calculator.ts`.

FinLit states that it is not registered with SEBI or AMFI. A written compliance review is required before a public pilot. Typed amounts stay in the browser unless a disclosed feature, with consent, needs them.
